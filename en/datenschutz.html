Privacy Policy.

Privacy policy of medeqon GmbH (hereinafter referred to as "medeqon")

As at 16/02/2026

1. medeqon's activities

medeqon operates in the following business areas:

  • Engineering office in the field of medical technology

Activities in this area include, among others: drawing up plans, advising clients, supervising construction sites

Trade in medical devices

Activities in this area comprise the purchase, sale and storage of products

Mechatronics technician for medical devices

Carrying out safety inspections on medical devices

Medical technology consulting

Business consultancy in the field of medical technology

2. Data subjects who are in contact with medeqon

Data subjects in this area include, among others: construction firms, architects' offices, client representatives of hospital facilities, owners of medical practices (in various medical specialisms)

3. Foundations of data protection law

Data Protection Amendment Act 2018

Data Protection Deregulation Act 2018

National competence lies with the Data Protection Authority

Definitions of terms:

  • The provisions of the GDPR apply to the processing of personal data of natural persons

Processing means: collecting, recording, storing, adapting, altering, retrieving, using, comparing, linking, erasing, destroying

Data of legal persons (e.g. OG, KG, GmbH) are not subject to data protection under the GDPR

Special categories of personal data are

Genetic data and biometric data

Health data

Data concerning a person's sex life or sexual orientation

Personal data revealing racial and ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs or trade union membership

"Profiling" means that certain data are collected in order to analyse personal aspects of a person. These data include, among others: health, economic situation, behaviour, location

4. Findings that apply to medeqon under the law currently in force

medeqon does not process any special categories of personal data!

medeqon does not carry out any profiling!

5. medeqon's principles of data processing

Lawfulness, fairness, transparency

Data must be collected lawfully and processed in a transparent manner

Purpose limitation

Data are collected and processed only for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes

Data minimisation

Data are to be limited to what is necessary in relation to the purpose of processing

Accuracy

Inaccurate personal data are erased or rectified

Storage limitation

The storage period is kept to a minimum. Data are used only for as long as they are needed

Integrity and confidentiality

Data are handled securely. medeqon ensures protection against unlawful use, loss or damage

Accountability

medeqon is responsible for compliance with the principles and can demonstrate such compliance

6. Under the GDPR, the processing of personal data is lawful if the following conditions are met

Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party, or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract (for example: invoicing address, names for invoicing)

Processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject

Separate consent of the data subject is not required for the two points mentioned above

7. Declaration of consent to medeqon

In all cases beyond those set out in point 4, medeqon obtains a declaration of consent

The declaration of consent contains the agreement to the processing of the personal data

medeqon's declaration of consent can be found in the appendix

8. Rights of a data subject from whom medeqon has collected data

Pursuant to sections 42-45 of the Data Protection Act (DSG), Chapter 2, Rights of the data subject

Transparent information and communication

Duty to provide information where personal data are collected from the data subject

Duty to provide information where the personal data have not been collected from the data subject

Right of access of the data subject

Right to rectification

Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)

Right to restriction of processing

Notification obligation regarding rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing

Right to data portability

Right to object

9. medeqon's responsibility

medeqon shall take appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure that the obligations of the GDPR are implemented in its operations

10. medeqon's technical and organisational measures

Data protection by default: only such data as are needed for order processing are obtained from clients, and no more. This applies in particular to the amount of data collected, their extent, their processing and the storage period

Declarations of consent are actively obtained from clients who do not meet the criteria set out in point 4

Clients' data are used exclusively for the performance of contracts and for compliance with legal obligations

11. Documentation obligation - maintaining the record

medeqon fulfils the documentation obligation by maintaining a record of data processing activities

12. Data protection officer

medeqon refrains from appointing a data protection officer for the following reasons:

  • The processing is not carried out by an authority or a public body

The core activity of the company does not require extensive, regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects

No special categories of personal data are collected

13. medeqon's notification obligation

In the event of a personal data breach, medeqon will report it to the Data Protection Authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach (in accordance with Art. 33 GDPR / Notification of a personal data breach)

Notification is not required where the personal data breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons

Where the personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the personal rights and freedoms of natural persons, medeqon will notify the data subject of the breach without undue delay

14. Data confidentiality

Personal data are to be kept confidential within the company

Employees may only transmit personal data to third parties on the basis of an express instruction

The provisions of the "Data confidentiality" section must be observed even after the termination of the employment relationship

15. Transfer of data to third countries or international organisations

Data traffic within the EU is not subject to any restrictions on account of the uniform level of data protection established by the GDPR

The transfer of data to third countries may only be carried out at medeqon if an adequacy decision exists on the basis of Data Protection Directive 95/46/EC

Where there is no adequacy decision by the Commission, the transfer must be authorised in advance by the Data Protection Authority

Exceptions apply in the following cases:

  • The data subject expressly consents to the transfer
  • The transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract between the data subject and medeqon
  • The transfer is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims

The transfer is necessary in order to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of other persons, where the person is physically or legally incapable of giving consent

16. Appendix - medeqon's data protection declaration of consent

The contracting party agrees that their personal data, namely:

  • Name and address of the recipient of the service
  • VAT number of the recipient of the service
  • Commercial register number
  • Telephone number
  • Email address

be processed by medeqon for the purpose of communication and the issuing of business documents and order forms (for example: invoices and delivery notes).

This consent may be withdrawn at any time by notifying medeqon (office@medeqon.com). The withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of the processing carried out up to that point.
